# Turn off hardware acceleration in your browser

[日本語](hardware-acceleration.ja.md) ・ **English**

> **If hardware acceleration stays on in your browser, the video area of your captures and recordings comes out completely black.**
> Turn it off before you use Shiori.

## How to turn it off

Restart the browser after you change the setting. It does not take effect until you do.

**Chrome**: Settings → System → turn off "Use hardware acceleration when available" (`chrome://settings/system`)

**Edge**: Settings → System and performance → turn off "Use hardware acceleration when available" (`edge://settings/system`)

**Vivaldi / Brave / Opera and others**: each has the same option in its system settings.

**Firefox**: Settings → General → Performance → turn off "Use recommended performance settings", then also turn off "Use hardware acceleration when available"

After turning it off and restarting, tick **I turned it off and restarted the browser** in the first step of **Set up Shiori**.
