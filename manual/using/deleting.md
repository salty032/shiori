# Deleting and undoing

[日本語](deleting.ja.md) ・ **English**

Select items in the list and press `Delete` to delete them. In the viewer, `Delete` deletes the item being shown.

When you delete many at once, Shiori asks you to confirm: "Delete {count} items? You can undo with Ctrl+Z within 15 seconds."

## Undoing

Press **Undo** in the notice that appears right after deleting.
The notice disappears after a few seconds, but you can still undo with `Ctrl+Z` for **15 seconds** after deleting.

> **After 15 seconds the files are deleted for good. They do not go to the Windows Recycle Bin.**
