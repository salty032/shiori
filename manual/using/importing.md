# Import your own images and videos

[日本語](importing.ja.md) ・ **English**

You can also add things to the library that you did not capture from a stream.

## Pasting

Copy an image to the clipboard, then press `Ctrl+V` in Shiori.

## Dropping

Drop image or video files, or a folder containing them, onto the Shiori window.
Only `.webm` / `.mp4` videos of 30 seconds or less can be imported.
Up to 200 items can be imported at once.

You can also drop onto the screen shown when the library is empty.

## How imported items differ

Imported items have no title, playback position or URL. Type a title in the details panel and use the notes if you need them.
You can step through an imported video one frame of the file at a time, but you cannot open a timesheet for it.
