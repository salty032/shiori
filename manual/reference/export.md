# Export formats

[日本語](export.ja.md) ・ **English**

There are two ways to get things out of Shiori: **Export**, which copies selected items as files, and **library export**, which moves your whole library.

## Export (selected items only)

Select items in the list, press **Export** in the details panel, and choose a destination folder.
Images are copied as the saved files.

The format for clips is set by **Export format** under Settings → Data → Video export.

| Format | What you get |
|---|---|
| webm (default) | Written as recorded |
| mp4 | Converted to H.264. Slight quality loss |

Clips that could not be converted to mp4 are written as the recorded webm instead, and the notice says so.

## Library export and import

**Export library…** under Settings → Data saves your captured images and clips, along with tags, notes and smart folders, to a folder.
Images you imported yourself are not included.

On another PC or a reinstalled Shiori, read that folder in with **Import library…** under Settings → Data.
**Importing the same folder twice gives you two copies of everything.**
