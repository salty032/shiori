# Reporting bugs

[日本語](feedback.ja.md) ・ **English**

Press **Report a bug or idea** from the "?" in the sidebar to open the report form in your browser.

The form already contains the Shiori version, OS type and Electron version.
It does not contain titles, URLs, images or anything like that, and nothing is sent from the app.

It helps a lot if you write which site, what kind of video, what you did and what happened.

**See what's new** under the same "?" shows what changed in the new version.
